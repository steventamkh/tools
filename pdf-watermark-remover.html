Notes
  1. Digital pages keep sharp, selectable text: only the watermark itself is taken out.
  2. Scanned pages are rebuilt as images with light shades turned white. A watermark darker than the writing, or one that covers the writing, cannot be fully separated.
  3. A digital page with a watermark that isn’t listed above can be added to the scan list. It will then be cleaned as an image.
  4. Use “Removed” in the preview to see exactly what will be taken out: red is removed, faded grey is kept.
