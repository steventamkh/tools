Page Extractor

Pick the pages you want from a PDF and save them as a new PDF, or remove the ones you don't want.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

1Choose a PDF

Click to choose or drag the PDF here

2Pick pages

Type numbers and ranges, or click the thumbnails below (Shift-click selects a run). In “Keep” mode, typed order is kept, so 5-8, 1 puts page 1 last.

3Download

One PDF
Separate files (.zip)
Notes:
  1. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged.
  2. “One PDF per range” makes a file for each comma-separated part you typed, e.g. 1-3, 7 gives two files.
  3. Password-protected PDFs need the password removed first.
