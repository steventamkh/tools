Page Organizer

Drag pages into a new order, rotate them and add blank pages, then download the reorganised PDF.

Files stay on your device — nothing is uploaded

Pages will appear here.

Drag a page to move it (on a phone or tablet, press and hold, then drag). Click to select · Ctrl/⌘-click or Shift-click to select several and move them together · double-click to view a page large. Blank pages go after the selected page, or at the end if nothing is selected.

Notes:
  • Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged. Rotation is stored in the PDF (no re-rendering).
  • Bookmarks and links between pages of the original file are not kept.
  • Password-protected PDFs can't be reorganised — open the file and "Print to PDF" first.
  • Shortcuts: R rotate +90°, Shift+R −90°, Del delete, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+A select all, Esc clear selection.
