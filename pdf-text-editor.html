PDF Text Editor

Click any line of text in a PDF and retype it. The tool reuses the fonts already inside the PDF, so the new text matches. Works for English, Chinese and numbers.

Fonts in this PDF
Most PDFs only carry the letters they already use. New letters come from another font in the PDF, the same font on your computer, or a look-alike.
How it works
  1. Click a highlighted line, type your change, press Enter. Unchanged letters stay exactly where they were.
  2. Letters that are already somewhere in the PDF are drawn with the PDF's own font. A message tells you if any letter has to come from elsewhere.
  3. Download the edited PDF. Text stays real text (searchable, selectable).
Limits: text doesn't re-flow into the next line; scanned pages and password-protected PDFs can't be edited.
