Notes
  1. “Keep original page size” gives the same result as printing the shorter pages to PDF, but keeps text sharp and selectable. Clickable links on cropped pages are not kept in this mode.
  2. With “Shorter pages”, choose “Fit to page” when printing on A4.
  3. In a digital PDF the trimmed text still exists inside the file, so this is not a way to redact confidential information.
  4. Trim sizes are in millimetres of the real page, so they work for pages of any size.
