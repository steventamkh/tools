Saving the PDF

  1. Click Save as PDF. Your browser's print window opens.
  2. Set Destination (or Printer) to Save as PDF.
  3. Under More settings, untick Headers and footers so the date and web address are not printed. Leave margins on Default — the page size and margins come from your file.

What to expect

  • This is a fast, in-browser converter, not a full LaTeX installation. Page breaks and spacing are close to LaTeX but not identical.
  • Diagrams (tikzpicture, circuitikz) are drawn by a small TeX engine that downloads the first time it's needed. Chinese text inside a diagram can't be drawn.
  • Not supported: pgfplots, BibTeX/biblatex (use thebibliography), headers and footers, and packages that need a real TeX run. Anything skipped is listed under Notes.
  • Images: PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF work. Convert EPS images to PDF or PNG first.
