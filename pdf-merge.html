PDF Merger

Combine two or more PDF files into one, in the order you choose.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

1Add PDFs

Click to choose or drag PDFs here Pick several at once. You can keep adding more.

2Arrange

    Drag the handle (or use ▲ ▼) to change the order. Leave “Pages” blank to use every page, or type e.g. 1-3, 5.

    3Merge & download

    .pdf
    Notes:
    1. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged.
    2. Pages keep their own size and orientation (e.g. A4 and A3 can be mixed).
    3. Password-protected PDFs need the password removed first.
